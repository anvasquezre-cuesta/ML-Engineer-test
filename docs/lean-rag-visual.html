02 / Retrieval

Shortlist fast. Decide carefully.

Vector similarity is good at finding related topics, but related is not always the same as answering the question. Reranking makes that final distinction over only a small candidate set.

03 / Scope

Advanced enough to help.

The design adds only what maps to a visible failure mode. It remains small enough to implement, test, and explain during a take-home review.

Implementation boundary

✓
Keep: section-aware chunks, about 150–300 words Split on headings, paragraphs, and complete list items.
✓
Keep: simple source and section metadata Document ID, filename, type, section, page range, chunk index.
✓
Keep: vector top 10 → reranker top 3–5 One extra model, loaded once and reused.
–
Skip: BM25, rank fusion, and parent-child indexes Useful at another scale, unnecessary for this corpus.
–
Skip: query rewriting and agentic loops More latency and behavior to test without a current need.
