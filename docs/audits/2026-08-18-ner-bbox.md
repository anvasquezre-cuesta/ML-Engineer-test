## Scope

What was checked, and how:

1. **Code review** of the three in-scope files, line by line, plus the modules they depend on for geometry
   (`ocr_service._build_page`, `text_normalization.normalize_name`) and the composition root that owns the
   spaCy model lifetime (`factory.py`, `api/dependencies.py`).
2. **Empirical end-to-end run** of the *real* pipeline (`build_extraction_service(get_settings())` → real
   Tesseract, real `en_core_web_sm`, real `OCRBoundingBoxService`) over all three `sample_pdfs/*.pdf`,
   then independent verification of every returned box with PyMuPDF: (a) box ⊂ `page.rect`, (b) re-render
   of the page region at the returned box and re-OCR of those pixels, (c) the same check with the box
   mirrored on the y-axis, to prove which vertical convention the numbers use.
3. **Adversarial/synthetic probes** with the real services: name split across a line break, PERSON entity
   that is a substring of a longer OCR token, unlocatable mention, ALL-CAPS names, titles, initials,
   accents, duplicate names, `/Rotate 90` page, page with a translated MediaBox.

> Environment note: `tesseract` is **not installed** on this machine (`which tesseract` → exit 1), so the
> extract path cannot run as-is here. For the audit I unpacked Ubuntu's `tesseract-ocr` 5.3.4 debs into
> `/tmp/tessdl/root` and put them on `PATH`/`TESSDATA_PREFIX`. Nothing in the repo, the venv or `uv.lock`
> was modified. This is an environment gap, not a code defect, but it is worth a line in the README.

## Verdict

**The graded geometry is correct and I found no P0 in this scope.** Boxes are emitted in PDF points
(not pixels), derived from the *actual* `page.rect` rather than an assumed Letter/A4 size, with a 0-based
page number; 56/56 boxes on the sample PDFs are strictly inside `page.rect` with positive width/height,
and 55/56 re-OCR of the returned region reproduces the name (median similarity 1.00, mirrored-y control
0.18) — a DPI scale error or a y-flip would have been caught by that test and was not present. Rotation
(`/Rotate 90`) and a translated MediaBox were also verified to produce boxes that land on the right pixels.
NER is correctly restricted to `PERSON`, the model name is configurable, the model is loaded exactly once
per process and injected behind a Protocol, offsets are page-relative, repeated occurrences each get their
own box, and matching is genuinely full-string/case/accent-insensitive (`fuzz.ratio` over
`normalize_name`), not a substring trick. What is *not* production-grade is everything around the model's
mistakes: nothing filters obvious non-person entities (4 of 56 outputs on the samples are headings,
`Forbes` and `Breakthrough`), an entity that spans a line break silently produces a union box covering
whole unrelated lines (measured: a 205.9 × 40.8 pt box over two heading lines), a name the model misses is
missed silently (occurrence recall 52/55 — `Elizabeth Park` lost on 2 of 3 occurrences), the fuzzy fallback
can relocate a box to a different occurrence on the page with no log line, and the vertical convention
(top-left origin, y-down, rotation applied) is documented nowhere even though the README's audit-viewer
bonus depends on it. Those are the P1s.

### 6. Performance / lifetime

```

spacy.load ner-only     : 0.44s  components=['tok2vec','ner']
full      per-document NER time: 52.6 ms   PERSON on p0: 13
```

Model lifetime is correct: `SpacyNERService.__init__` (`ner_service.py:28`) loads eagerly, the only
production construction site is `factory.build_extraction_service` (`factory.py:17`), and that is behind
`@lru_cache(maxsize=1) get_extraction_service` (`api/dependencies.py:16`) — one load per process, reused by
every request, and injectable (`nlp` parameter) for tests. A missing model raises `NERModelLoadError`
(`ner_service.py:39`), which the dependency turns into a clean 503 (`dependencies.py:22-27`); because
`lru_cache` does not memoise exceptions, a later request retries the load, which is the behaviour you want.

## What is already good

- **The graded coordinate trap is genuinely handled.** Points, not pixels; scale derived from the real
  `page.rect` (`ocr_service.py:134-135`) rather than an assumed page size; clamped to the page rect
  (`:170-173`), so every box is inside the page by construction; 0-based page numbers enforced twice, by
  `OCRDocument.__post_init__` (`domain.py:83-86`) and by `BoundingBox.page_number: Field(ge=0)`.
- **Offset-first localisation is the right design.** `OCRPage.text` is assembled from the very words that
  carry the boxes (`ocr_service.py:182-212`), NER runs per page so entity offsets are page-relative
  (`ner_service.py:55`), and `_words_for_span` (`bbox_service.py:96-105`) recovers the exact words by
  interval overlap instead of re-searching text. That is why the fuzzy fallback almost never fires
  (0 fallbacks needed across 56 mentions) and why every occurrence of a repeated name gets its own box
  (`test_bbox_service.py:66`, confirmed live: `Margaret Thompson` twice on `company_memo` p0 at y=98.88 and
  y=535.68).
- **No dedup, correctly.** Each occurrence is one `ExtractedName`, which is the only behaviour compatible
  with "each occurrence has its own box".
- **Matching is full-string and normalisation is right.** `_similarity` uses `fuzz.ratio` over
  `normalize_name` (`bbox_service.py:171-177`) — no `partial_ratio`/substring tricks — and
  `normalize_name` (`text_normalization.py:9-19`) does NFKD + combining-mark strip + `casefold` +
  punctuation folding, so `María González` ≡ `MARIA GONZALEZ` ≡ `Maria  Gonzalez`, and `O'Brien` /
  `Al-Rashidi` fold consistently on both sides.
- **Clean seams.** `SpacyNERService` and `OCRBoundingBoxService` take `Settings` plus an injectable
  collaborator, depend on nothing global, are constructed in one composition root, and are consumed through
  `Protocol`s (`protocols.py:26-41`) that the tests assert with `isinstance` — the pipeline is testable
  without a model and without HTTP.
- **Everything tunable is configuration.** `ner_model_name`, `bbox_match_threshold`,
  `bbox_max_horizontal_gap_factor`, `ocr_dpi` are all `Settings` fields with `DOC_INTEL_` env overrides and
  are mirrored in `.env_example` — no magic numbers in the two services.
- **Typed, meaningful errors and no silent excepts.** `errors.py` gives a small hierarchy; every `except`
  logs with context and re-raises a typed error; the one silent path (a mention that cannot be located)
  at least logs a WARNING with page and offsets.
- **Domain models carry real invariants.** Frozen `slots=True` dataclasses; `OCRWord` validates confidence
  range, non-empty text and non-empty spans; `OCRPage` cross-checks word page numbers and offsets against
  the text; `OCRDocument` enforces contiguous 0-based pages — which is exactly what makes "page 0 must be
  OCR'd" impossible to regress silently.
- **Structured, useful logging** in both services (page, counts, durations) without dumping document
  content into the logs.
